How to use {{ title }}

Tap a topic to open it.

Moving the camera
  • Arrows: press and hold to move; let go to stop. Diagonals work too.
  • Speed: Slow / Med / Fast under the arrows. Slow is easiest for landing on a person.
  • Zoom − / +: hold to zoom out or in; let go to stop.
  • HOME: jumps to {% if home %}{{ home }}{% else %}the first preset{% endif %} ({% if active_ward %}the {{ active_ward }}'s first preset{% else %}the first preset{% endif %}).
  • Grid lines on the preview help frame shots (rule of thirds). The # button at the bottom-right of the preview turns them on or off; each phone remembers its choice.
  • The preview refreshes a few times a second. If it freezes, the camera or Wi-Fi dropped out; the controls may still work.
Presets and wards
  • Tap a preset to send the camera to that saved position.
    • {% if wards %}
  • Pick your ward in the box at the top of the Presets card. The buttons change to your ward's own presets; each ward has its own saved positions.
  • The ward choice is for every phone: if you change it, other operators' phones switch too within a couple of seconds, and it stays set after a restart.
  • No ward shows the original chapel presets.
    • {% endif %}

Saving a preset position
    {% if wards %}
  1. Pick the right ward first (saving a ward preset only affects that ward).
    2. {% endif %}
  2. Aim and zoom the camera exactly how you want the shot.
  3. Tap Edit in the Presets card. The buttons get a dashed outline.
  4. Tap the preset to overwrite and confirm. You'll see Saved ….
  • Edit mode turns itself off after one save, so a stray tap can't overwrite anything else. Tap Done to leave without saving.
  • Saving replaces the old position for everyone using that preset. Every save is recorded in the server log.
  • After a save, check that your next move works. If the camera ignores it, just press again.
Zoom meeting buttons
  • TOGGLE AIR turns Zoom video and microphone on or off together. Red means you're on air; green means off air.
  • Video only / Mic only toggle just one.
  • These press Zoom's own shortcuts (Alt+V, Alt+A) on the host PC, so the Zoom meeting window must be the active window there. If it isn't, you'll see “Zoom not focused” and nothing is sent: click the Zoom window on the PC.
What the status pills mean
ON AIR / OFF AIR Whether both video and mic are live. “(live)” is read from Zoom itself; “(assumed)” is the app's best guess.
VID ON MIC OFF Video and mic separately.
● live The app can see Zoom's real mute/video state. Nothing to do.
toolbar hidden In a meeting, but Zoom's toolbar has hidden itself. On the PC, enable Zoom Settings → Meetings & Webinars → Controls → Keep meeting controls visible.
no meetingZoom isn't in a meeting yet.
UIA off The app can't read Zoom's state on this PC; states are assumed. Toggles still work.
Zoom focusedZoom is the active window; toggles will work.
⚠ notepad.exe Another program is in front of Zoom on the PC. Click the Zoom window.
last 10:42:07When a Zoom toggle was last sent from any phone.
Something isn't working
  • Page won't load / “network” warnings: make sure the phone is on the chapel Wi-Fi, not mobile data. The controller runs on the host PC, which must be on and logged in.
  • Camera doesn't move: check the preview updates. If it's frozen too, the camera may be off or restarting (it can take a minute after power-up).
  • A preset goes somewhere odd: it may never have been saved. Aim the camera and save it (see “Saving a preset position”).
    • {% if wards %}
  • “…isn't one of the selected ward's presets”: someone changed the ward on another phone. Pick your ward again.
    • {% endif %}
  • Screen keeps going dark: tap anywhere once after the page loads; the app then keeps the screen awake while it's open.
  • Tip: use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” to open the controls full-screen like an app.
